# Deployment

## Local development

Load `extension/manifest.json` from Firefox's `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox` page.

## Packaging

Zip the contents of `extension/` so `manifest.json` is at the archive root. Store packages outside the source tree or under the ignored `web-ext-artifacts/` directory.

## Publication

Mozilla Add-ons signing and store publication are out of scope for the MVP and requires a separate release review.
